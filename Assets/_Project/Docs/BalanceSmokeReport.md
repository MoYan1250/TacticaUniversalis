# Dust II quick simulation — first balance smoke report

Generated from canonical content with seeds 1–1000 after the first anti-snowball
calibration.

- Matches: 1,000
- NAVI wins: 581 (58.1%)
- Gambit wins: 419 (41.9%)
- Average match length: 24.23 rounds
- Average losing score: 8.10
- Overtime rate: 4.8%
- Matches where the loser scored at most 2: 87 (8.7%)
- Matches where the loser scored at least 12: 252 (25.2%)
- Average simulation events per match: 627.3

These numbers are a smoke test, not a final balance target. They confirm that
matches terminate, neither team has an extreme deterministic advantage, and
the initial economy/form snowball has been reduced. Card content, tactical-plan
variety and live player feedback should be added before setting final targets.
